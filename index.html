RACING TIPS
  • W gas, A D steer. Ease off the gas before a corner, and tap Space to drift round a tight one.
  • Q E punch left / right, F kick. Come up fast behind a rider to knock them off.
  • In a Power Race the crates hold powers (oil, smoke, bombs, EMP, hook, rocket, warp): press G to use the one you hold.
  • Grab the coloured crates (N nitro, S super speed, D shield, P power hits) and ride over the orange arrows for speed.
  • Gold coins buy new bikes in the Garage. Hit people or crash and the police chase you.
